How it works

Three phases.
One through-line.

📋
Phase one
Prep

Before you walk in, InAble builds your social brief — personalized starters, a clear goal, and a preview of who'll be there. You go in with a plan, not a prayer.

💬
Phase two
Coach

Live, in the moment. InAble surfaces what to say next — deepen, open, rescue, or exit — based on what's actually happening. Glanceable on your watch, tappable in your pocket.

📈
Phase three
Debrief

After. You write it out. InAble reads it — surfaces what worked, what to try next, and catches the moments you're still replaying at midnight.

Flow controls

Your energy.
Your pace.

+
Increase flow

Ready to go deeper? Or bring someone new into the conversation? The + button gives you one specific, immediately usable move — a question that unlocks real talk, or a natural intro that makes you the connector.

"I feel like we've only scratched the surface — what's something you rarely get to talk about at these things?"
−
Decrease flow

Running low? The − button gives you a graceful redirect — hand the floor, invite someone else in, or become the curious listener. You stay present without having to perform.

"I'm curious what everyone else thinks — [Name], you've dealt with this more than anyone here."
